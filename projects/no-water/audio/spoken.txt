How long can you survive without water?
Your body is about sixty percent water, and you lose some every minute just by breathing and sweating.
After one day, you feel thirsty, tired, and get a headache.
After two days, your mouth dries up, your skin wrinkles, and your heart beats faster to push thicker blood.
By day three, your kidneys start shutting down and your brain gets confused.
Most people can only survive about three days without water. So drink up!
