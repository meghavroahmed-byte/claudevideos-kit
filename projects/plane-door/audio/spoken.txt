What if you opened a plane door mid-flight?
Good news, you can not! At cruising height, the air inside pushes on the door with the weight of about seven cars.
The door is a plug, bigger than its frame, so the pressure seals it even tighter.
But if a hole did open, loose things would get sucked out, and oxygen masks would drop.
you would have less than a minute before passing out. So put on your own mask first!
