What if you got lost in the desert?
In the day, the sand can heat up to seventy degrees. that is hot enough to fry an egg!
Your body sweats out about a liter of water every hour, just trying to stay cool.
So do not walk in the midday sun. Rest in the shade, and travel at night when it is cooler.
And beware of mirages. That shimmering lake ahead? it is just light bending over the hot sand.
