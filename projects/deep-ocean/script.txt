What if you dove to the bottom of the ocean?
At ten meters, the water already squeezes you twice as hard as the air above.
At two hundred meters, the sunlight is gone. It's cold and pitch black.
At one thousand meters, glowing creatures light up the dark.
At four thousand meters, the pressure is like an elephant standing on your thumb.
And at the very bottom, eleven kilometers down, it would crush you flat in an instant.
