How does a chameleon really change color?
Most people think chameleons change color to blend in. But that's mostly a myth.
Their skin has tiny crystals called nanocrystals. When a chameleon gets excited, it stretches its skin, and the crystals move apart.
That changes how light bounces off them, turning the skin from green to yellow, orange or even red.
So their colors are mostly about mood, temperature, and showing off to other chameleons!
