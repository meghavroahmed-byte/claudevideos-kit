How many times do you blink in a day?
About fifteen times a minute. that is around fifteen thousand blinks a day!
Each blink lasts about a third of a second. Add them up, and your eyes are closed for over an hour every day!
Blinking wipes tears across your eyes like a windshield wiper, washing away dust.
But when you stare at a screen, you blink way less. that is why your eyes feel dry!
