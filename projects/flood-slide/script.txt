What should you do if you get trapped inside a flooding water slide? Most people panic and try to climb backwards, but the ultra-slippery walls make that impossible. The secret is to stay completely calm. First, tilt your head back to breathe from any remaining pockets of air. As the water keeps rising, try using your hands to propel yourself down the tube. If you're completely jammed, just brace yourself and let the slide fill up entirely. Once the water pressure equalizes, you'll be able to easily push forward and float out to safety.
