What would happen if you got stuck in quicksand?
In the movies, people sink all the way under. But in real life, that's impossible.
Your body is less dense than quicksand, so you'd only sink up to your waist.
The real danger is getting stuck. Pulling your foot out fast takes as much force as lifting a car!
So don't panic. Lean back, spread your arms, and wiggle your legs slowly.
Water seeps in, the sand loosens, and you float free.
