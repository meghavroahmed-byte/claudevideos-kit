Why do fingernails grow faster than toenails?
Your fingernails grow about three and a half millimeters a month. Your toenails? Less than half that.
Nails grow from a root under your skin, and that root loves a good blood supply.
Your hands are closer to your heart, and you use them all day. Tapping, typing, and scratching.
All that action helps your fingernails grow more than twice as fast!
