What happens to your body if you stop sleeping?
After 24 hours, you feel like you're drunk. Your reactions slow down, and your mood crashes.
By day two, your brain starts shutting off for a few seconds at a time, even with your eyes open. These are called microsleeps.
On day three, you may start seeing things that aren't there.
The world record for staying awake is about 11 days.
But don't try it, because your brain needs sleep to clean itself every night.
