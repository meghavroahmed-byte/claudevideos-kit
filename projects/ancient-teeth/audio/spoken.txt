How did people brush their teeth five thousand years ago?
Ancient Egyptians made toothpaste from crushed eggshells, ashes, and powdered ox hooves.
They brushed with chew sticks: twigs with frayed ends, chewed until they turned into tiny brushes.
The Chinese later made the first bristle toothbrush from stiff hog hair tied to a bone handle.
And the Romans? Some rinse their mouths with urine, because its ammonia helped whiten teeth!
