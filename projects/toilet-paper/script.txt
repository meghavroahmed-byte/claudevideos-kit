What did people use as toilet paper before toilet paper existed?
Ancient Romans shared a sponge on a stick, called a tersorium, and rinsed it in salty water for the next person.
The Greeks used smooth pieces of pottery, sometimes with their enemies' names written on them.
Farmers used corn cobs, leaves, and moss. Vikings used wool.
And real toilet paper? It only showed up in the 1850s.
So next time you run out, just be glad you're not borrowing a Roman's sponge!
