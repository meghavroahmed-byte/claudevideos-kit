What if you stared at the Sun?
Your eyes lens works like a magnifying glass. It focuses sunlight into a tiny, burning hot spot on the back of your eye.
And the scary part? Your retina has no pain nerves, so you will not even feel it burning.
Hours later, you might notice a blurry blind spot right in the middle of your vision. And it can be permanent.
So never look straight at the Sun, not even during an eclipse!
