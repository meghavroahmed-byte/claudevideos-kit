What would actually happen if a sperm whale swallowed you whole? First, you'd be tightly squeezed down its giant throat and pushed through the esophagus. From there, you'd drop into a series of stomach chambers completely flooded with powerful digestive acids that would instantly begin dissolving your body. Yet, even if you miraculously survived the acid, you wouldn't last long — the total lack of oxygen inside the whale's stomach would cause you to suffocate almost immediately.
