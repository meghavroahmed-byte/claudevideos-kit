What if you stepped into space without a spacesuit?
You wouldn't explode like in the movies.
Instead, the air would rush out of your lungs, so never hold your breath.
After about 10 seconds, the water in your mouth and eyes starts to boil, and your skin swells up like a balloon.
After about 15 seconds, you'd pass out from lack of oxygen.
But here's the surprising part: if you're rescued within about 90 seconds, you could still survive!
