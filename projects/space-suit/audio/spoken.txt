What if you stepped into space without a space suit?
You wouldn't explode like in the movies.
Instead, the air would rush out of your lungs, so never hold your breath.
After about ten seconds, the water in your mouth and eyes starts to boil, and your skin swells up like a balloon.
After about fifteen seconds, you'd pass out from lack of oxygen.
But here's the surprising part: if you're rescued within about ninety seconds, you could still survive!
