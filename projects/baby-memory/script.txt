Why can't you remember being two years old? Back then you learned something new every day. But your brain was still under construction. The hippocampus, the part that files away memories, wasn't finished yet. New brain cells kept sprouting and rewiring it, scrambling the old files. You also had no words for what happened, and no clear sense of "me." So those memories were made... they just got lost. Most people's earliest memory starts around age three.
