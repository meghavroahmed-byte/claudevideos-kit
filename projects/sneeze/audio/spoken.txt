What happens when you sneeze?
When dust or pollen tickles the inside of your nose, nerves send an alarm to your brain.
Your brain fires back instantly. You take a deep breath, your chest muscles squeeze, and your eyes close on their own.
Then, boom! Air blasts out of your nose and mouth at up to one hundred sixty kilometers per hour.
A single sneeze can spray thousands of tiny droplets more than six meters away. So always sneeze into your elbow!
