What was life like for a Roman gladiator?
Most gladiators were slaves or prisoners, but some were free men who signed up for fame and money.
They trained every day at special schools, and ate a diet of barley and beans to build a layer of fat that protected them from cuts.
And unlike in the movies, most fights didn't end in death. Gladiators were expensive, so a fallen fighter was often spared.
And the crowd helped decide his fate.
