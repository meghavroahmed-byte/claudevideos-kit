Could you outrun a tsunami?
In the deep ocean, a tsunami travels as fast as a jet plane, over eight hundred kilometers per hour.
Near the shore, it slows down to around thirty to fifty kilometers per hour. That's as fast as the fastest human sprinter, and most of us are much slower.
And the water doesn't stop. It keeps flooding inland, carrying cars, trees and debris.
So don't try to outrun it. Run uphill, or climb a tall, strong building as soon as the ground shakes or the sea pulls back.
