What if a mosquito was as big as a human?
Its wings would beat hundreds of times per second, as loud as a helicopter.
Its needle-like mouth, called a proboscis, would be as long as your arm.
And one bite could suck out liters of blood in seconds!
Luckily, that could never happen. Insects breathe through tiny tubes, and at that size, they couldn't get enough oxygen to survive.
