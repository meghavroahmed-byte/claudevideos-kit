What if you could float like a duck? First, your belly would puff out like a built in life jacket, and your spine would bend backward so you sit upright instead of tipping forward. Your legs would slide toward your back, turning into paddles that keep you steady. Inside, you would grow air sacs, little balloons that make your body lighter for its size. Then thousands of feathers would cover your skin, trapping warm air and keeping the cold water out. Put it all together, and you would bob on the water just like a duck.
