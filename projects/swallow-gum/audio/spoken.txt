What really happens if you swallow chewing gum?
Some people say it stays in your stomach for seven years. That's a myth!
Gum is made of a rubbery base, plus sugar and flavor. Your body absorbs the sugar and flavor, but your stomach can't digest the gum base.
Still, it doesn't get stuck. It slides through your intestines with everything else, and leaves your body in a few days.
But swallowing lots of gum at once can cause a blockage, so it's still better to spit it out.
