Why should you never try to pet a hippo?
They look slow and chubby, but hippos are one of the most dangerous animals in Africa.
A hippo can run up to 30 kilometers per hour on land, faster than most people.
Its jaws open nearly 180 degrees, and its bite is strong enough to snap a small boat in half.
And they're very territorial. If you get too close to their water, they will charge.
So admire them from far away!
