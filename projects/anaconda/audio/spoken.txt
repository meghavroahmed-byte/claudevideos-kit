What if you got swallowed by a green anaconda?
First, it wouldn't bite you to death. It would wrap around you and squeeze, tighter every time you breathe out, until your heart can't pump blood.
Then its jaws stretch wide, and it pulls you in headfirst, inch by inch.
Inside, there's no air, and powerful acids start to work. It could take the snake weeks to digest you.
Luckily, anaconda almost never eat humans. We're just too wide at the shoulders.
