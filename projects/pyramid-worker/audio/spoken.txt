What was a day like for the workers who built the pyramids?
They weren't slaves. Most were paid workers who lived in a village right next to the pyramid.
They woke up at sunrise and worked in teams of about twenty people.
Using copper tools, they cut huge limestone blocks, some weighing more than two tons.
Then they dragged them on wooden sleds, pouring water on the sand to make them slide easier.
And their pay? Bread and beer. Lots of beer!
