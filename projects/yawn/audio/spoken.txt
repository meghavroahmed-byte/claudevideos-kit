Why are yawns contagious?
When you yawn, your jaw stretches wide and you suck in a huge gulp of air.
Scientists think it helps cool down your brain, like a fan for your head.
But here is the weird part. Just seeing someone yawn can make you yawn too.
Your brain copies the people around you, especially friends and family.
Even reading about yawning works. Are you yawning right now?
