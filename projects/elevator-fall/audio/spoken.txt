What would happen if your elevator cable snapped?
Don't worry, you wouldn't plummet to the ground. Elevators hang from several steel cables, and each one can hold the full weight on its own.
Even if every cable broke, automatic safety brakes would clamp onto the rails and stop the car.
And at the bottom of the shaft, giant springs or oil buffers soften any landing.
In fact, elevators are one of the safest ways to travel!
