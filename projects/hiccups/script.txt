Why do we get hiccups?
Under your lungs is a dome-shaped muscle called the diaphragm. It moves down every time you breathe in.
Eating too fast, drinking fizzy drinks, or even laughing too hard can irritate it, and it suddenly spasms.
You gulp in air, and a flap in your throat slams shut. That's the hic sound!
Most hiccups stop on their own within a few minutes. But one man in America hiccupped for 68 years!
