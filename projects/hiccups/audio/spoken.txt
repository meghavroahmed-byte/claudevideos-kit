Why do we get hiccups?
Under your lungs is a dome shaped muscle called the diaphragm. It moves down every time you breathe in.
Eating too fast, drinking fuzzy drinks, or even laughing too hard can irritate it, and it suddenly spasms.
You gulp in air, and a flap in your throat slams shut. That's the hick sound!
Most hiccups stop on their own within a few minutes. But one man in America hiccup for sixty eight years!
