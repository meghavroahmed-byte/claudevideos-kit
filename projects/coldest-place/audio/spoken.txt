What would happen if you stood outside in Antarctica at minus ninety degrees?
Your breath would freeze into tiny ice crystals the moment it left your mouth.
Within a minute, any bare skin would start to get frostbite.
Your body would shiver hard to make heat, and your blood vessels would squeeze to keep your organs warm.
Breathing that air could even damage your lungs.
That's why scientists there wear thick layers, goggles and face masks, and never stay outside for long!
