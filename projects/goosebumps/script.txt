Why do we get goosebumps?
When you get cold, tiny muscles at the bottom of every hair on your skin pull tight.
The hairs stand straight up, and the skin around them pops up into little bumps.
For our furry ancestors, this puffed up their fur to trap warm air. Animals still do it to look bigger when they're scared.
That's why a scary movie, or your favorite song, can give you goosebumps too!
