What if you fell into a black hole?
As you get close, gravity pulls much harder on your feet than on your head.
Your body gets stretched longer and thinner, like spaghetti. Scientists really call this spaghetti vacation!
To a friend watching from far away, you would seem to slow down and freeze at the edge, fading red.
But for you, time keeps going, straight into the darkness. And nobody knows what is inside.
