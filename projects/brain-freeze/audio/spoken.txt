What is brain freeze?
You take a big bite of ice cream, and suddenly, a sharp pain stabs your forehead.
The cold hits the roof of your mouth, right where a lot of blood vessels sit.
They shrink from the cold, then quickly swell back up to warm things again.
That rush of blood sets off pain nerves, and your brain thinks the pain is in your head.
The fix? Press your tongue on the roof of your mouth, and it fades in seconds.
