What if you fell through a frozen lake?
The icy water shocks your body. You gasp, and your heart races.
Don't panic. Control your breathing for the first minute.
Turn back the way you came. That ice already held you, so it's the strongest.
Put your arms on the ice, kick like a swimmer, and slide out on your belly.
Then roll away, and don't stand up until you're far from the hole.
