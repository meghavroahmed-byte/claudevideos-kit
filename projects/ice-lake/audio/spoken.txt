What if you fell through a frozen lake?
The icy water shocks your body. You gasp, and your heart races.
do not panic. Control your breathing for the first minute.
Turn back the way you came. That ice already held you, so it is the strongest.
Put your arms on the ice, kick like a swimmer, and slide out on your belly.
Then roll away, and do not stand up until you are far from the hole.
