What actually happens when a bee stings you?
A honeybee's stinger has tiny barbs, like a harpoon. Once it goes into your skin, it gets stuck.
When the bee flies away, the stinger rips out of its body, along with a venom sac that keeps pumping venom for up to a minute.
That's why you should scrape it out quickly, instead of squeezing it.
Sadly, the honeybee dies after stinging. But wasps have smooth stingers, so they can sting again and again!
