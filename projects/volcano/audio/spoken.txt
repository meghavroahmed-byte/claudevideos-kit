What would happen if you fell into a volcano?
First of all, you wouldn't sink. Lava is about three times denser than water, so you'd land on top of it like a rock on mud.
But at over one thousand degrees Celsius, the heat would hit you long before you touched it.
Your clothes would catch fire, and toxic gases would make it almost impossible to breathe.
That's why volcano scientists wear heat-proof suits and never get too close.
