How did knights go to the toilet while wearing armor?
A full suit of armor weighed around twenty five kilograms, and it took a squire up to half an hour to put on.
So knights couldn't just take it off every time nature called.
The trick? Plate armor didn't cover everything. Underneath, knights wore padded clothes and a chain mail skirt that could be pulled aside.
But in long battles, some knights simply had to hold it... or not!
