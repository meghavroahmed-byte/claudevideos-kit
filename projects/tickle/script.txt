Why can't you tickle yourself?
When someone else tickles you, your brain can't predict where their fingers will go next. That surprise sets off your laugh.
But when you try it yourself, a part of your brain called the cerebellum predicts every move before it happens.
It tells the rest of your brain: relax, it's just me. So the tickle feeling gets cancelled out.
Fun fact: some people with certain brain conditions can actually tickle themselves!
