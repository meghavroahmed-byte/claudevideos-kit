Why does hair grow?
Under your skin, every hair sits in a tiny pocket called a follicle.
At the bottom, cells divide super fast and push the older cells up and out. Those dead cells harden into the hair you see.
It grows about a centimeter a month. But each hair only grows for a few years, then falls out, and a new one starts.
That's why you lose up to a hundred hairs every single day!
