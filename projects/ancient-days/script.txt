What did people do all day in ancient times when there were no jobs, offices, or even money?
Well, they definitely weren't sitting around doing nothing!
Most people spent their days hunting animals, gathering fruits, fishing, and searching for clean water.
Others built shelters, made tools, prepared food, and protected their families from wild animals.
As farming developed, people started growing crops, raising livestock, and building permanent communities.
And here's the surprising part: surviving back then was basically a full-time job!
