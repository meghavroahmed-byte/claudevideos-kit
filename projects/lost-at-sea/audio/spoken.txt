What would happen if you were lost at sea on a tiny raft?
Day one: the sun burns your skin, and you're already thirsty. But never drink seawater. The salt makes you even more dehydrated.
Day two: you feel weak and dizzy. Collect rainwater any way you can.
Day three: your body is running out of energy. Fish can keep you alive.
And the best way to get rescued? Stay with your raft, and use a mirror to flash sunlight at passing ships.
