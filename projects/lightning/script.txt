What happens to your body when you get struck by lightning?
A single bolt carries up to 300 million volts, and it's five times hotter than the surface of the Sun.
In a split second, the current races over your skin, burning your clothes and even blowing off your shoes.
It can stop your heart and leave fern-shaped scars called Lichtenberg figures.
But here's the surprising part: about 90 percent of people struck by lightning survive!
