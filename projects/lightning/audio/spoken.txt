What happens to your body when you get struck by lightning?
A single bolt carries up to three hundred million volts, and it's five times hotter than the surface of the Sun.
In a split second, the current races over your skin, burning your clothes and even blowing off your shoes.
It can stop your heart and leave fern shaped scars called like ten berg figures.
But here's the surprising part: about ninety percent of people struck by lightning survive!
