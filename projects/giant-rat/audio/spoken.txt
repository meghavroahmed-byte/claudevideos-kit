What if rats were as big as you?
A rats front teeth never stop growing, about twelve centimeters every year.
So it chews nonstop, through wood, plastic, even concrete.
Blown up to your size, those teeth could bite straight through a car door.
A normal rat can survive a fall from five stories high.
But a giant one? Its skinny legs could not carry its own weight, and it would starve trying to feed that huge body.
