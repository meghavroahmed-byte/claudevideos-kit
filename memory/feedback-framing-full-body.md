On 2026-11-18, about the sperm-whale short, the user said: don't open by zooming in on the person's face. Open with the person swimming and the big whale behind them, then the swallow, then going inside. They also said: "you zoom in so much that I only see the body going into the stomach, not the head; videos shouldn't be this zoomed in."

**Why:** the viewer must always see what is happening to whom. Tight crops on a torso or a face hide the action and the character.

**How to apply:**
- Hook = a medium or wide shot that shows the situation in the first frame: the character's full body and the threat or setting together. No face close-ups as the opener.
- Keep the whole character, head included, in frame in every shot. At 9:16, a 1.8 m body needs roughly 3 m+ camera distance. Angle the camera so a lying or horizontal body runs top-to-bottom in the frame, head at the top.
- Use a close-up only briefly, for one specific detail, and never one that cuts off the head.
- Check every still for this before rendering.
