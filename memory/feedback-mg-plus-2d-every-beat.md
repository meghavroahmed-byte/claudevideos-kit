This does not undo [[feedback-motion-graphics-not-cartoon]]: full-screen cartoon is still rejected. The 2D sits INSIDE the MG layout, in a framed card or circle window, and is animated (people walking, digging, hunting, reacting), not a static picture.

- Every beat gets an MG layer plus a 2D animated window acting out that line.
- The user's character (their rig) appears regularly through the video, about every 3–5 beats (pointing, reacting, explaining) in a framed window or corner pip. Not just once at the intro.
