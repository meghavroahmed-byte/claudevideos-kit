The user said that using the same person in every video ("hero" = RenderPeople Nathan) looks cheap and boring, and that the videos feel static: the person stands in one place, the camera barely changes, scenes don't change, and nothing else is happening.

**Why:** Zack D. Films-level shorts feel alive: different characters, many locations, constant camera motion and cuts, and lots happening in the background.

**How to apply (every future short):**
- Use a NEW / different main person per video (vary gender, age, look, clothes). Don't default to `person('hero')`. Fetch more rigged humans (RenderPeople free CC-BY "Rigged"/"Animated" on Sketchfab, Mixamo-compatible) into `_assets/sf/` when the library runs out. The MR. CLOUD print is not required on every person.
- Several scenes/locations per video (a new place for each beat where the script allows), not one set for 30 s.
- The camera is always moving: push-ins, orbits, tracking, low/high angles, plus hard cuts every ~2-4 s. No locked-off shots of a person standing still.
- The person must act: walk, run, react, interact with props. Not idle in one spot.
- Fill the scene: background people, animals, moving props, weather/particles, so the world feels alive.
